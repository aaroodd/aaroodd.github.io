Sterile neutrinos with keV-scale masses are compelling dark matter candidates. My work investigates how non-standard neutrino self-interactions modify the viable parameter space for sterile neutrino production in the early Universe.

### Key results

- Introduced an effective field theory (EFT) framework for neutrino self-interactions affecting dark matter production
- Identified new viable regions of sterile neutrino mass and mixing parameter space
- Derived constraints relevant to upcoming experiments: TRISTAN, ECHo, BeEST, HUNTER

I work at the intersection of particle physics, cosmology, and data — trying to understand what the Universe is made of and how it got here.

My research focuses on **neutrinos** and **dark matter**, specifically how non-standard interactions in the early Universe affect the production of sterile neutrino dark matter. I also spend time thinking about statistical methods, signal extraction, and how to pull faint patterns out of noisy data — skills that travel well between a physics experiment and a trading floor.

When I'm not chasing ghost particles, you'll find me watching markets, building things in Python, or reading about quantitative strategies.
